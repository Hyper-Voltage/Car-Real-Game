A quick start

Learn to drive a manual

Start with F. Steer with A D. Choose your pedals:GAS and STEER on both sides. Use opposite hands. CLUTCH and BRAKE help you stop and shift; the gear lever is in the middle.

Hold Space for the clutch. While holding it, tap or drag the lower-right shifter, use 1–6, or E/Q. Release Space gently and add W for gas.

Three ways to shift: the number keys, E/Q up/down, or the fixed H-pattern shifter. Clutch in first. N neutral · R reverse.

Hold CLUTCH, drag the central lever through neutral into first, add GAS and ease the clutch out.
HOLD keeps the clutch pressed while you shift. The horizontal channel is neutral; R is reverse. Tap fullscreen for more room.

New to manual driving? The guided tutorial teaches starting, clutch control, steering, shifting, stopping, reversing and parking in a quiet practice drive.

You can also tap or drag the lower-right H-pattern while holding the clutch. Mouse shifter (on the menu, in Pause or in Settings) captures the mouse: moving it operates the H-pattern continuously, without holding a button or aiming at the widget. Hold left-click for clutch before moving into a gear, right-click for gas, and Space for brake. Return through neutral before crossing to another gear rail. Escape releases the mouse and pauses; Resume captures it again. Shift also holds the clutch.

Touch

GAS and STEER are on both sides: hold gas with one hand and steer with the other. The blue CLUTCH disengages the engine; BRAKE slows the car. Hold the clutch and drag the central gear lever out of its gear, through neutral and into the next slot. Slide the clutch up for a slow release; slide brake or gas up for more. HOLD latches the clutch while you move the lever. Simplified shift buttons and a hood camera are optional in Settings.

Semi: shift from 5th to 6th

Tap HIGH 6–10 beside the shifter, press CLUTCH, and move the lever through neutral. The labels change to 6–10: select 6, in the same slot as low-range 1, then ease the clutch out. A queued change waits for neutral. To return to gears 1–5, choose LOW and pass through neutral again.

Keyboard: clutch in, press 6 for sixth (0 for tenth); number keys select the range automatically. G toggles the range for the physical lever. E/Q, controller shifts and touch +/− cross between fifth and sixth automatically. The guided tutorial includes a parked range-change practice for both semis.

Automatic

W goes, S or Space brakes, A D steer. The engine starts itself, the gears change themselves, and the car waits at a stop until you press the gas. R switches between reverse and drive.

Stalled?

The engine is off, so the gas does nothing. Hold the clutch and press F (START on touch), or press N for neutral and then F. Then pull away again as below. On Guided, F alone does it.

Pulling away

  1. Press the clutch and start the engine.
  2. With the clutch down, pick first gear.
  3. Add a little gas, then let the clutch out slowly. The engine note drops and the car starts to roll at the bite point.
  4. Once you are moving, let the clutch out fully and accelerate.

    A free manual driving simulator in your browser

    Clutch & Shift puts you in the driver's seat of the Kestrel Vanta GT, a fictional sporty hatchback with a six-speed manual gearbox. The engine, clutch and gears are simulated together, so the car behaves the way a stick shift does: rev it in neutral and nothing moves, let the clutch out too fast at idle and it stalls, start off in third and it bogs, drop down a gear and the revs climb while engine braking slows you.

    Pick your vehicle

    Six manuals share the same clutch and gearbox physics, each with its own engine, gearing and handling: the front-drive Vanta GT hatchback, the Apex V8 sports car with a seven-speed, the RZ Turbo tuner with boost that builds from about 3,000 rpm and a blow-off valve, a 1972 half-ton pickup with a four-speed and a granny-low first gear, and a semi truck with a ten-speed range box (gears 1-5 in low range, 6-10 in high), driven bobtail or hauling a loaded 53 ft trailer that can jackknife. Every vehicle can be driven from the left or the right seat.

    How the clutch works here

    Holding the clutch disconnects the engine from the wheels. As you let it out, it starts to grip at the biting point: the engine note drops slightly and the car begins to creep. A little gas plus a slow, steady release gives a smooth launch. On a keyboard the clutch comes up along a gentle curve when you let go of Space (or left-click in Mouse driving), and you can press the clutch again at any moment to pause the release. On touch screens you can slide the clutch pedal for fine control.

    Where you can drive

    • Downtown: a grid of streets with working traffic lights, crosswalks, four-way stops and the Main Street Roundabout at 2nd Avenue. Yield to circulating traffic and follow the arrows counterclockwise.
    • Maple Heights: quiet residential streets with a steep hill and a stop sign right on the slope, perfect for practicing hill starts with the parking brake.
    • Loop 1 Expressway: a three-lane highway loop with an on-ramp, a merge lane and an exit, long enough to settle into fifth and sixth.

    Challenges

    Five short challenges score what the car actually did: First Gear (clean starts), Smooth Operator (smooth shifts and stops), Rush Hour (stop-and-go traffic), Highway Run (merge, cruise and exit) and No Stall (one stall ends the run). Shifts are judged by how closely the engine speed matched the wheels when the clutch bit, and by the jolt it caused.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can this teach me to drive a manual car?
    It is a game, not driving instruction. It can help you understand what the clutch, gears and revs are doing, but practice in a real car with a qualified instructor.
    Does it work on phones and tablets?
    Yes. Gas and steering are available on both screen sides for two-hand driving, with larger tablet targets, a central H-pattern shifter, and clutch and brake on the left. A guided tutorial walks you through starting, shifting, stopping, reversing and parking. Optional shift buttons, a hood view and clutch assist provide an easier setup.
    Can I use a game controller?
    Yes. Xbox-style controllers work with analog triggers for gas and brake and LB for the clutch.
    What do the difficulty levels change?
    Guided releases the clutch more gently, resists stalling and suggests gears. Standard is the intended experience. Realistic has a sharper clutch, stalls more easily, brakes harder on the engine and makes you release the parking brake yourself on hills.
